# PUI89 Quadruped Screening Robot — Video Concept Storyboard

**Format:** 60–75 second concept film, 16:9, realistic high-tech airport terminal.  
**Status:** Storyboard / production plan, not a rendered video. All detection graphics are illustrative.

## Shot list

| Time | Shot | Motion and visual direction | On-screen message |
|---|---|---|---|

| 67–75 s | Closing hero shot | Robot waits calmly beside a marked route as passengers pass. | Simulation concept only • no real narcotics detection |

## Visual and sound direction

- Photorealistic, clean industrial design: dark graphite chassis, restrained teal status lighting, visible depth/LiDAR sensor head, four articulated legs with rubber feet.
- Natural airport lighting, glass reflections, realistic human scale, gentle camera dolly and tracking; no aggressive chasing or blocking.
- Sound: soft terminal ambience, quiet actuator movement, subtle non-alarm notification, calm operator voiceover.
- Keep all overlays abstract and non-identifying. Do not depict a robot seeing through clothing or detecting substances inside a body.

## Accuracy and safety labels

This is a concept storyboard. The quadruped is not a validated physical robot. The chemical sensor, scan overlay, and alert are illustrative only; no real narcotics detection or screening performance is demonstrated. A simulated alert must be marked **“Inconclusive — authorised human review required.”** No facial recognition, demographic profiling, autonomous accusation, detention, or enforcement.
