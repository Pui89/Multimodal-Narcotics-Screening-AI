# Product brief — Multimodal Narcotics Screening AI

**One-line description:** A local-first, human-supervised research workflow for evaluating authorized screening evidence, uncertainty and multimodal inputs.

## The problem
Screening systems must handle incomplete, noisy or conflicting observations and must not confuse an AI score with proof of a substance's chemical identity. Reliable evaluation requires lawful data, qualified ground truth, calibration and a safe path to human review.

## What the project provides today
- Local API/dashboard and audit-oriented workflow components.
- Evaluation utilities and synthetic regression fixtures.
- Data provenance, manifest and group-aware split tools.
- Documented uncertainty and human-review principles.

## What is not yet a product claim
The current dashboard's sample values are manually entered; image preview is not a trained vision model. Independent real-data performance, multimodal benefit, chemical identification and validated physical screening-robot capability have not been established. The prototype must not independently trigger enforcement decisions.

## Potential first customers and collaborators
Academic evaluation labs, authorized screening-technology integrators and research groups studying trustworthy multimodal systems. These are audiences to validate, not confirmed customers.

## Why evaluate it
The research direction makes missing evidence, conflicting inputs, abstention and auditability explicit. Pharmaceutical pill images are not a substitute for data representing the intended illicit-substance screening task.

## Demonstration plan
1. Demonstrate synthetic examples with a clear synthetic-data label.
2. Show how missing or conflicting inputs result in UNKNOWN and human review.
3. Explain data rights, ground-truth requirements and the intended-use boundary.
4. Publish held-out metrics only when authorized, representative data are available.
5. Obtain independent legal, privacy and domain-expert review before a pilot.

## Evidence required before a pilot
- Narrow written intended use and documented authorization.
- Lawful, expert-labelled data and leakage-resistant splits.
- Independent held-out evaluation, calibration and error analysis.
- Privacy/security controls and documented abstention tests.
- Independent expert/legal review and confirmatory testing procedures.

## 30 / 60 / 90-day targets
- **30 days:** define the lawful task and data governance requirements.
- **60 days:** audit available data and strengthen uncertainty/fault tests; do not claim model accuracy without suitable data.
- **90 days:** complete a research evaluation protocol and expert review plan. Dates are targets, not promises of completion.

## Short award pitch
Multimodal Narcotics Screening AI explores trustworthy screening workflows in which data quality, uncertainty and human review are first-class requirements. Its current contribution is an auditable software and evaluation scaffold; the next phase must establish lawful data access, expert ground truth and independent performance evidence before operational claims.
