# Legacy Screening Archive — Not Current Sentinel Scope

The repository previously contained a separate Multimodal Narcotics Screening AI project direction. The current product is PUI89 Sentinel — All-Terrain Exploration & Inspection Robot, a four-legged quadruped for supervised exploration and inspection.

All screening-specific modules, datasets, schemas, dashboards, model adapters, tests, workflow files, demos and artwork are historical artifacts unless explicitly reviewed and reclassified. They are not current Sentinel features and must not be used to claim quadruped perception, locomotion, inspection or field capability.

## Handling rules

- Preserve history and legacy files during migration.
- Do not run screening applications or workflows as Sentinel demonstrations.
- Do not transfer former screening metrics, evidence credits, model outputs or readiness percentages to the new product.
- Do not delete or mass-rename legacy files without a file-level dependency review and reversible plan.
- Generic utilities may be reused only after separate safety, security, licence, provenance and test review.
- Active product documentation and public pages must describe quadruped exploration and inspection only.

See [Sentinel Scope Migration](SENTINEL_SCOPE_MIGRATION.md) for the current migration status and completion checklist.
